Site Name

Nancienan Tech Consultancy

This name combines "Nancienan" which is a name dedicated to the memory of my mother, Nancy Nanthuru, with "Tech Consultancy" to clearly communicate the organization's purpose — providing technology training and consulting services to the rural community of Domasi, Zomba.

Domain: nancienantech.mw

(.mw is the country code top-level domain for Malawi, reinforcing the local identity)

Site Purpose

The website serves as the official online hub for Nancienan Tech Consultancy, a coding school dedicated to empowering rural youth in Domasi, Zomba, Malawi. The site provides:

  • Information about the courses offered: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and GitHub
  • A platform for youth to learn how technology can solve local community problems
  • An online application form for prospective students to register
  • A progress tracking tool to help students monitor their learning journey
  • Resources and motivation for youth to become self-starters and create their own programs and websites

The ultimate goal is to bridge the digital divide, reduce risky behaviors among youth, and create economic opportunities through technology.

Scenarios

Questions that rural youth and community members might ask when visiting the site:

  • "What courses do you offer and how long do they take?" — A visitor wants to know what skills they can learn and the time commitment required. The Courses page will provide details on HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and GitHub, including duration and what each course covers.
  • "How can learning to code help me find a job or start my own business?" — A young person wants to understand the practical benefits of coding. The site will explain how coding skills enable youth to become self-starters, design their own programs, build websites, and create solutions for local problems — leading to financial independence and entrepreneurship.
  • "How do I apply to join the program?" — A potential student wants to know the enrollment process. The site features a simple online application form where they can register with their personal details and course preferences.
